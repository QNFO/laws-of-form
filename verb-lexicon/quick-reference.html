Quick Reference

All 13 verbs at a glance. Click any verb name to jump to its full entry.

Part I: Verbs of Attention

How the system looks at the world.

VerbDefinitionSociety's Noun
Scanning Wide-aperture threat-detection; attention sweeps the environment, tuned to friction, change, or ambiguity. The Anxious Person, The Worrier
Locking Fixation; the perceptual field collapses to a single point, dropping all peripheral context. Driven / Obsessive (target-dependent)
Drifting Uncoupling; attention refuses to land on the immediate physical environment. Energy-saving disengagement. The Slacker, The Daydreamer

Part II: Verbs of Friction

How the system behaves when it hits a wall.

VerbDefinitionSociety's Noun
Forcing Linear pressure; treating the environment as a solid object to be battered down through sheer willpower. The Hero, The Fighter or The Stubborn Fool (outcome-dependent)
Spinning The loop; high effort, zero distance traveled. Rehearsing, circling, chewing on undigestible input. The Neurotic, The Overthinker
Bracing Rigid boundaries; the system tenses up and violently rejects outside inputs to maintain its shape. The Avoidant, The Lone Wolf

Part III: Verbs of Relation

How the system interacts with other systems.

VerbDefinitionSociety's Noun
Merging Permeable boundary; the system destabilizes its own baseline to match whatever it is near. The Codependent or The Empath
Echoing The mirror; energy bounced directly back — a perfectly symmetrical reaction bypassing internal state. Reactive, Thin-Skinned or Charming
Tilting Leverage; the subtle shift of context rather than the application of force. The quietest verb. Rarely named. Invisible.

Part IV: Verbs of Narrative

The stories the system runs to make sense of raw data.

VerbDefinitionSociety's Noun
Projecting The "If/Then" story; the system tethers to a future condition, and when achieved, projects onto the next. The Ambitious or The Never-Satisfied
Fixing Pathology; the system treats itself as a broken machine requiring endless maintenance. The Self-Improver, The Seeker
Performing The mask; constant internal surveillance, terrified that the real self will be exposed. The Impostor, The Fraud or The Charismatic
Architecting Building frameworks and refusing to dwell in them. The builder who never moves in. The Architect, The Systems Thinker

By Frequency of Naming

FrequencyVerbs

The Meta-Verb

Noticing

Pattern recognition by a pattern recognizer. The only lever. Not a pattern-we-mistake-for-a-person, but the capacity that sees all the others as verbs.

Noticing is not in the lexicon proper because it is not a pattern mistaken for an identity. It is the ground on which the lexicon becomes readable. You cannot tilt what you cannot see. The first verb is always Noticing.
