## ADR-0002: Differentiation from ultrametric-tree-universality

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-05-23

### Context

Due diligence revealed that `ultrametric-tree-universality` (Archive 2026/05) covers the same core topic (ultrametric tree universality, Spencer-Brown, molecular clock) and produced a publication-ready document. The primordial-mark project must differentiate to avoid duplication.

### Decision

We will differentiate by focusing on:
1. **The primitive itself** — formal definition of the Mark as mathematical primitive (not just survey of where trees appear)
2. **Classification of Finite Simple Groups connection** — how CFSG emerges from the Mark (unique to this project)
3. **10-adic / positional ontology** — the "Be 10 by virtue of zero position" insight (unique to this project)
4. **Synthesis across all 5 threads** — producing a unified framework rather than a domain survey

We will NOT re-survey the 8 domains already covered in ultrametric-tree-universality 0.3.md.

### Consequences

- **Easier:** Clear unique contribution, avoiding duplication
- **Harder:** Must carefully reference but not repeat prior work
- **Risks Accepted:** Risk of insufficient differentiation if the synthesis doesn't go deep enough into the primitive

## ADR-0004: Prior Work Differentiation — Full Review

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-05-23

### Context

Due diligence identified 3 prior projects with thematic overlap. S2 content audit confirmed significant overlap with ultrametric-tree-universality. S3 completes the review by examining all three projects in depth.

### Findings

#### 1. ultrametric-tree-universality (2026/05)

**What it is:** "Where the Tree Breaks" — a catalog of ultrametric breakdowns across 8 domains (spin glasses, QCD jets, phylogenetics, p-adic geometry, linguistics, cognition). 43,746-char publication with 6 sections + 2 appendices. Publication-ready with YAML frontmatter, curly quotes, zero bare Unicode.

**Key thesis:** Edge-count distance on a rooted tree is automatically ultrametric (theorem, not discovery). The Compton frequency supplies a physical floor. Ultrametricity is BOTH fundamental constraint AND selection bias.

**Overlap with primordial-mark:** 0.1.1.md duplicates the 8-domain survey. Both reference Spencer-Brown and Zitterbewegung.

**Differentiation strategy:** primordial-mark focuses on primitive definition and CFSG connection; references but does not re-survey domain breakdowns.

#### 2. Syntactic Generation Primitive Distinctions (2026/04)

**What it is:** "The Unified Autaxic Ontology: Syntactic Relational Patternism" — a mature framework (v3.0) integrating the "Generative Engine" of early Autaxys research with Ultrametric Physics, Ratio-Based Valuation, and Token Calculus. Has a 244K-char publication (0.4.md) and a 679K PDF.

**Key thesis:** Reality is not composed of substances but of relational patterns generated by primitive syntactic distinctions. Rejects "Noun/Verb confusion of substance-based physics." Formal token calculus defines operations on distinctions.

**Overlap with primordial-mark:** Both center on the concept of a "generative engine" driven by primitive distinctions. Both descend from Spencer-Brown.

**Differentiation strategy:** Syntactic Generation is broader (full ontological framework with token calculus, ratio-based valuation). primordial-mark is narrower and deeper on specific mathematical structures (CFSG, 10-adic hierarchy, ultrametric trees). The token calculus could be BORROWED as formal foundation for primordial-mark's "Mark as self-embedding operator."

#### 3. Quantum Laws of Form (2026/04)

**What it is:** Direct application of Spencer-Brown's Laws of Form to quantum mechanics. Core thesis: "Foundational physics and logic do not require variables, indices, or numbers. The universe computes itself through the structural cancellation of boundaries (geometry)." 264K-char publication (0.8.md). Also synthesizes W-Boson mass tension with token calculus.

**Key thesis:** Quantum information isn't fragile — we've been measuring it wrong. The particle IS the boundary. Measurement problem dissolves into a labeling error. Topological qubits (knot theory, anyons, Chern-Simons) are the natural realization.

**Overlap with primordial-mark:** Both draw from Spencer-Brown. Both use "the Mark" / "boundary" as fundamental. Both frame reality as structural/geometric rather than substance-based.

**Differentiation strategy:** Quantum Laws of Form applies Laws of Form to physics (quantum mechanics, TQFT). primordial-mark applies it to mathematics (CFSG, ultrametric trees, number theory). Different domains, shared root. Quantum Laws of Form's crisp "particle IS boundary" framing could strengthen primordial-mark's ontological claims in §I.

### Decision

We will differentiate primordial-mark from all three prior projects by:
1. **Not re-surveying** domain breakdowns (ultrametric-tree-universality owns this)
2. **Not building** a full ontological framework or token calculus (Syntactic Generation owns this)
3. **Not deriving** quantum mechanical consequences (Quantum Laws of Form owns this)
4. **Focusing uniquely** on: (a) formal definition of the Mark as self-embedding operator, (b) the CFSG-as-taxonomy critique, (c) the 10-adic positional ontology, (d) Spencer-Brown re-entry as the generative engine of mathematical hierarchy

We may BORROW from prior projects:
- Syntactic Generation's token calculus → formal foundation for the Mark
- Quantum Laws of Form's "particle IS boundary" → crisp ontological framing
- ultrametric-tree-universality's Compton clock → reference, not re-derive

### Consequences

- **Easier:** Clear lane — we own the primitive definition + CFSG connection; prior work handles the rest
- **Harder:** Must carefully cite prior work without duplicating; must be explicit about boundaries
- **Risks Accepted:** The Mark→CFSG bridge remains undemonstrated (G2 from S2 audit). Prior projects don't solve this either — it's genuinely novel territory.
