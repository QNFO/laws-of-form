## Thesis

The "Mark" — Spencer-Brown's primordial act of drawing a distinction — is not merely a logical primitive but a generative engine. Every distinction branches the tree. The infinite variety of patterns generated by recursive application of the Mark IS the ultrametric tree. The Classification of Finite Simple Groups, the 10-adic numbers, the Compton/Zitterbewegung clock — all are exhaust of this single, infinite, recursive engine.
