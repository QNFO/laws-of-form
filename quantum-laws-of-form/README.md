## Abstract

Quantum information is not intrinsically fragile; we have been measuring it incorrectly. This monograph derives the Standard Model particle spectrum (mass, charge, spin) as projective cross-ratios of irreducible syntactic patterns, models spacetime as a Bruhat–Tits tree with passive geometric fault tolerance, and explains cosmological data (CMB temperature, log-periodic oscillations) as the coarse-grained shadow of a discrete, ultrametric reality. Testable predictions include log-periodic oscillations in the CMB, passive fault tolerance in non-Archimedean quantum circuits, and ultrametric clustering in neural data.
